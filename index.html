3. Start order after the power goes out

Motors only surge for about a second. Start them one at a time, biggest surge first, and a smaller generator can carry the same house.

    How the math works

    • Running total: the sum of running watts for everything on at once.
    • Staggered peak: running total plus the largest single surge, meaning starting minus running watts for the biggest motor.
    • Recommended rating: running watts at least running total ÷ 0.8, so the generator stays under about 80% load for hours, and surge or starting watts at least the staggered peak.
    • Most submersible well pumps are 240 V. You need a generator with a 240 V outlet and a transfer switch or interlock installed by an electrician. Never backfeed through a dryer outlet.
    • Soft-start or constant-pressure drives (for example, variable-speed submersible controllers) cut the surge. If you have one, set starting W close to running W.
